aromanticism & asexuality

my experience

i don't remember how or when i stumbled upon the aroace spectrum, but i identified with it starting from when i was like 14-15 of age iirc. i dated an allo person for a few months, insisting i was on the spectrum and still felt romantic attraction for them, but now i'm sure that i didnt. i think i just felt exitement and platonic attraction. sensual drive was also a factor i think, as this was a bit after the initial covid lockdown and even in my close bubble, we didnt hug as much as we used to. i feel bad, like i used that person to experiment or something, but i genuinely thought i liked them romantically and didnt understand why i felt uncomfortable with their pda.

i don't remember when i stopped using the aroace label either. i think i started calling myself queer or just gay mostly because of dysphoria - if people saw me as a gay boy they maybe wouldnt think twice of my feminine features or whatever. starting from when i was 12 ive always known my identity was queer in some nature, and i love and accept that part of myself. i think i just didnt accept the fact that ill never have a romantic relationship. always hoping that maybe one day, ill feel it. i still hope this, but i know it probably never will happen. i have a lot of unlearning of aphobia and amatonormativity to do.

my asexuality has been the easiest to accept i think bc im still pretty young and people like. dont expect me to have sex i guess? idk. i had more or less hoped my entire orientation would change once i started t and had top surgery bc i had heard of that happening to other people. and of course that can happen once youre more comfortable in your body and all that. i know identities can change. mine hasn't, at least not my aroace identity. i identified as sapphic for a long time and then bi and then aroace and then queer and now.

im not really out as aroace i think. i told one (maybe two? i dont remember help) friend at school. my cousin vaguely knows but also kinda not, and maybe some other friends i dont have all that much contact with. i came out to my parents as bi when i was 14 because i thought thatd be easier to explain. "expect anything". this month (june 2026) my little brother asked me who im into and i was like "haha i dont have time for that!" and then my mom suggested to him that maybe i dont like anyone like that. so maybe shes onto me (or she read my journal again). i think id like to let my close family know about this but i want to figure it out more first. get to know myself and accept myself before i tell them.

ngl with making these pages i discovered some more terms and learned more about terms that describe my experience really well. i had seen the romance & sexuality stances online sometimes, but now i actually recognise myself in them. and i know there are people out there that feel the same way that i do, that im not alone.

pro tip what helped me figure myself out a little more was just make a list of things i want, things i think id like or things when i think about it i get nauseous. you can add more categories lol,, like i know i want to hug and cuddle with people. kissing i think id like but absolutely no tongue etc etc. it felt like doing a psychological test on myself but sometimes maybe its more important to know your boundaries than your labels, i guess?

conclusion of the day: im gonna keep on calling myself queer and aroace (when i feel comfortable specifying). i think the greyromantic label fits well but iiiiii dont know. aroace feels better at the moment.
