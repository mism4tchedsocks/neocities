about the trip

the trip was very fun. it was at a little house weve been to before, in an area in france where there isnt that much to do and everthing that there is to do, we did a few years back. but that didnt matter, it was too warm to do anything anyways. it had a shared pool, and I SWAM!!! THREE TIMES!!! each time early in the morning or late in the evening so there wasnt too much sun on my scars (yes yes i did it shirtless, which felt awesome but also weird in a "im naked" way), with my mom so i didnt get too anxious, and i fucking did it. im so proud of myself. it was so so so fun.

i read a bunch!! started raven boys and gave up by chapter two, it was very cliche and "ooh she hates him but shes gonna love him by the end" and not my thing! then i read "verzwegen verlangen", a book about gay and lesbian history in belgium. suuuper interesting, since i barely know anything about it. usually all the queer history you see online is american and maybe british. i also read "trans, et alors?", which was a bunch of interviews with trans people in belgium, some famous, some not. it came out this year and my parents bought it immediately. its nice to read experiences. i wish some were more in depth but i understand that its very personal and intimate sometimes. my parents are gonna give the book to my grandparents to borrow, in the hopes theyll understand more. they still misgender me sometimes, though its gotten better since starting t.
then i also read persepolis 1 by marjane satrapi, which was really good. im waiting for the other 3 to become available in my library so i can read them all. and last but not least i read pardalita by joana estrela, another graphic novel. also really really good. love me some coming of age queer fiction.

about today neighbourhood barbecue today! it was mostly elderly people. i was a bit anxious to go because uh oh what if i get misgendered etc etc. i keep forgetting that i pass pretty well now. and i still forget that my tits are gone. i just look like some guy now, albeit a young guy. someone thought i was 14 T-T. she did say i was very handsome though. lmao sometimes you just need a 80something woman to tell you you look good.
another benefit of passing: as a girl i usually was the person people asked to hold babies or play with the young kids. which was fine! i liked doing that, i still do, and i still get to do that with family friends and all that. but now a guy came to me to ask if i wanted to play football with his son. ME. i dont think i look like i play football, but in a town like mine, if youre a guy you play football. thats just the way it is. as a kid i enjoyed football at school but i wasnt often allowed to play (my year was very gender separated - the girls thought boys were icky and the boys thought girls were stupid). i dont think i was any good anyways, but i liked it! and now im still not any good but im older so the kids dont want to tell me im shit at it. fucking awesome. i won btw. nevermind that my opponent was 8 years old. I WON.
