 main goals & plans to achieve them.
  • reduce anxiety:
    write my thoughts down more and rationalize them or get "feedback" (?) on them. do things tired & scared. schedule regular meetings with my student counsuellor and psychologist (don't wait until i think i need to make an appointment). do at least one (1) scary thing every week.
  • artistic play:
    have fun, make more, don't think too much, HAVE FUN. don't get lost in a spiral of how to do it right (there is no right way) or how to be a good artist (there is no good or bad). spend more time in the studios outside of class.
  • be active in my local queer community:
    in the city at home, but mostly in my dorm city. already said i'm down to help organise a protest march. go to events.
  • walk more:
    i will go on a walk with my parents in my dorm area, so i can get to know the area with my safe people and hopefully feel comfortable enough to go on walks on my own during the year. take pictures during them. sketch during them. spot birds during them.
